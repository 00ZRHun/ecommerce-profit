See where your profit is leaking

  • You grow revenue to grow profit — but profit doesn't follow.

    Ad costs, discounts and fees absorb the upside. Your bank account doesn't match your dashboard.

  • You don't know your true contribution margin per order.

    ROAS looks fine. The P&L tells a different story. Without unit economics, every scaling decision is a guess.

  • Discounts and promos have trained your customers to wait.

    Revenue spikes, margin doesn't. Full-price sell-through falls and next month starts further behind.

  • Every decision still runs through you.

    Your team executes, but no one owns the profit number. You become the bottleneck for every test and trade-off.

One margin point matters more than you think. On $250k per month, a single point of net margin is $30,000 a year in pure profit — no extra traffic, no extra ad spend, no extra team.
