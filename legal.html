Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Profit Guild ("we", "us") provides profit-focused coaching and advisory services to ecommerce businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. You can reach us by email at the Guild team.

Information you give us

When you apply for a profit audit, subscribe to profit tips, or contact us, we collect the details you choose to provide. This typically includes:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your store URL, platform and monthly revenue range.
  • Your biggest business challenge and any goals or notes you add.
  • The program you are interested in, if you select one.

Information we collect automatically

We record basic, aggregate interaction data so we can see which parts of the page are useful — for example which sections people read, whether the calculator is opened, and how far down the page visitors scroll. This data is stored on our own server and is tied to a random session identifier, not to your name or email address. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Our web server also keeps standard request logs, which include IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. When you submit an application we store a one-way, daily-salted hash of your IP address and your browser's user-agent string alongside it, so we can detect duplicate or automated submissions without keeping your raw IP. The hash cannot be reversed into your address and changes every day.

Where your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we stop collecting this interaction data for your visit.

How we use your information

  • To review your application and respond, usually within 24–48 hours.
  • To prepare relevant profit opportunities for your store.
  • To send the profit tips you explicitly asked to receive.
  • To improve this site, our content and our services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We rely on your consent for marketing email, and on legitimate interests for responding to enquiries and keeping the site secure. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Confidentiality

Store data, account access and commercial information shared during an engagement are treated as confidential. We sign an NDA before reviewing sensitive business information. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Cookies & analytics

Last updated: 20 October 2026

We do not use advertising cookies

This site does not load third-party advertising, retargeting or social pixels, and it does not set cookies for tracking purposes. That is why you will not see a cookie consent banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

What we do use

  • First-party interaction analytics. Anonymous, cookie-free events sent to our own server (see "Information we collect automatically" above). Disabled automatically when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.
  • Local browser storage. If you start the application form, run the profit check or use the profit calculator, we save your inputs in your own browser's local storage (keys prefixed pgc_) so you do not lose your work if you refresh. This never leaves your device unless you submit the form, and you can clear it at any time.
  • Session identifier. A random value created for the current visit and kept in your browser's session storage. It groups anonymous events into one visit, is not a cookie, and is discarded when you close the tab.

Controlling tracking

You can enable "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control in your browser settings and our analytics will stop recording your visit. You can also clear your browser storage at any time to remove saved form drafts and calculator inputs.

Accessibility

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone who needs help with ecommerce profitability to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification or reduced-motion settings. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

What we have done

  • Semantic HTML landmarks, one main heading per page, and a logical heading order.
  • A "skip to content" link and full keyboard operability, including visible focus states.
  • Dismissible overlays that trap and return focus, and that close with the Escape key.
  • Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA for text and interactive elements.
  • Animations and reveal effects disabled automatically under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Labelled form fields, inline error messages announced to assistive technology, and errors that do not rely on colour alone.
  • Meaningful alternative text for informative images and decorative images hidden from assistive technology.
  • Charts and comparisons backed by real text and table data rather than images alone.
  • The site works without JavaScript: content is readable, navigation works, and forms submit normally.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any blocking accessibility issues, but interactive charts and sliders are the hardest areas to make perfect. If any part of this site does not work for you, that is a bug we want to fix. We test with current versions of Chrome, Safari and Firefox, and with keyboard-only navigation.

Feedback

If you hit an accessibility barrier, email the Guild team with the page and what happened. We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 5 working days and to provide the information you need in another format if the site is blocking you.
